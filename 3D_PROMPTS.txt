RoboPets - prompts for AI 3D generators (Meshy, Tripo, CSM, Rodin, etc.)
=====================================================================
Use one prompt per model. Ask for a GLB, T-pose (robot) or a clean side view (vehicle),
PBR materials, game-ready topology. Both are ORIGINAL designs - keep them free of any
existing franchise logos, names or signature features.

--- BULWARK (robot mode) -------------------------------------------------------------
Original heavy-duty mech robot, stylized hard-surface cartoon style with clean ink-like
edges. Broad, blocky silhouette, about 3.5 m tall, T-pose. Deep crimson armor plates over
dark graphite and gunmetal mechanical joints, brushed-steel details. Chest made of two
angled crimson plates with a glowing amber energy core visible in the gap between them.
Crimson helmet with a graphite center crest fin, silver face plate, angry V-shaped glowing
amber visor, small round steel ear pods. Huge crimson shoulder pauldrons shaped like truck
wheel-arch fenders, with a chunky off-road tire mounted on the outside of each shoulder.
Forearms carry flat crimson armored shields (truck doors). Thick legs with crimson thigh
and shin armor, an off-road tire on the outside of each thigh and each calf (6 tires
total). Backpack made of a truck cab roof, slanted windshield and a roof light bar with
amber lamps. A forearm-mounted orange plasma blade on the right arm (glowing, emissive).
Feet with crimson toe caps and small thruster nozzles on the soles.

Original low wedge-shaped mid-engine sports coupe, stylized cartoon hard-surface, about
